## 0.1.0

 - Initial release, moved out of the `ndk` package. It depends on neither `ndk` nor `ndk_core`. `ndk` depends on it and re-exports it.
 - `CachedRelayInfoRepo` wraps a `RelayInfoRepo` to fetch the info of a relay once and then serve it from memory. An answer older than `maxAge`, if given, is served while the relay is fetched again in the background. It is the cache `Connectivity` of `ndk` kept until now, which `ndk.connectivity.getRelayInfo` still goes through. It also remembers a relay without a document. A fetch that throws serves the last answer of the relay, throws only when it never answered, and is asked again on the next call or once `failureMaxAge` passes. `ndk` sets it to a minute. `evict` and `clear` forget relays. It ages answers with a `Stopwatch`, which a test can replace with `clock.stopwatch()`.
 - `CachedRelayInfoRepo` takes a `RelayInfoCache` to keep the answers across restarts, as `RelayInfoRecord`s that carry the url, the document or none, and when it was fetched. It reads one on the first call for its relay and serves it, and fetches it again in the background once `maxAge` has passed since that fetch, measured with the system clock. A call does not wait for the write. `evict` and `clear` return a `Future` and forget the relays in that cache too. `CacheManager` of `ndk_cache` implements `RelayInfoCache`, and `ndk.connectivity` gives it `NdkConfig.cache`.
 - **BREAKING** `RelayInfoHttpRepoImpl(httpDS: http)` is now `HttpRelayInfoRepo(http)`.
 - **BREAKING** `HttpRelayInfoRepo.getRelayInfo` returns null only when the relay answers without a document. It throws when the relay could not be reached (a timeout, a network failure, a 408, a 429 or a 5xx), and throws an `ArgumentError` for a url that is not ws, wss, http or https. It reads at most `maxBytes`, 1 MiB by default.
 - `RelayInfo`, `RelayLimitation`, `RelayFees` and `RelayFee` have a `toJson` that `fromJson` reads back, public constructors, and an `==` that compares their documents. The fields they do not read stay in `extra`, which `toJson` gives back, and a field they read is dropped from the `extra` given to a constructor. Their lists and `extra` are read-only, the nested values of `extra` included.
 - `RelayInfo.nips` lists the numbered NIPs first and in order, then the lettered ones, without duplicates, so the order a relay lists them in does not change `==`.
 - `RelayInfo.fromJson` reads a field with an unexpected type as null, a number that is not an integer included, so a relay that serves `1e400` cannot make it throw. It reads the document and its nested objects from any `Map`, such as one a Hive box gives back.
